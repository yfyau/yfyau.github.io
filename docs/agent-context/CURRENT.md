## Remaining risk and follow-ups

- The ten possible backend projects do not yet have a runtime/state/traffic/uptime inventory. Do not infer that they can share Workers, Containers, Railway, a database, or one VPS merely because they share a domain.
- Cloudflare Workers are request-driven rather than ordinary servers. Cloudflare Containers provide a full runtime but require Worker-controlled lifecycle/scaling. Railway's $5 Hobby price is a minimum that counts toward usage, not a flat price for ten always-on services.
- A shared VPS can lower the bill only after compatible workloads are measured; it also creates patching, backups, secrets, monitoring, capacity, noisy-neighbour, and shared-outage ownership. Do not pre-provision it for hypothetical demand.
- Prefer one first-level public hostname per product and same-origin `/api/*` routing. Free Cloudflare Universal SSL on this full zone covers the apex and first-level subdomains, not an assumed deeper hostname such as `api.project.yfyau.com`.
