Practical AI Transformation

Make AI useful in the work you already do.

I find where AI can save time or improve your service, redesign the process, and build practical tools and automations you can use day to day.

THE IDEA

Start with a real bottleneck. Then decide whether AI belongs in the solution.

Where it helps

A good fit when...

  • Your team repeats the same steps across inboxes, spreadsheets, or systems.
  • Useful knowledge is scattered, and finding an answer takes too long.
  • You have an AI idea, but need to work out where it would actually help.

How I work

From problem to useful workflow.

  1. Understand the work.Map the task, the people involved, the handoffs, and where time or accuracy is lost.
  2. Choose the right approach.Decide what to simplify, what to automate, and where AI adds value over a simpler tool.
  3. Build and refine.Create a focused solution, try it in the real workflow, and account for the exceptions people actually face.

The goal

A practical change your team can understand and use, with the work around it considered as carefully as the AI itself.
