From Prototype to Product

Your idea works. Now make the software hold up.

I review a fast-built prototype or an existing system, fix fragile parts, and make it more dependable and easier to maintain as it grows.

THE IDEA

Keep what proves the idea. Strengthen what the next stage depends on.

Where it helps

A good fit when...

  • A quick prototype has users or potential, but changes feel risky.
  • Recurring errors, confusing data, or manual fixes are slowing the team down.
  • You need someone to assess what is fragile and help improve it, not just deliver a report.

How I work

Find the weak points. Fix the important ones.

  1. Understand what matters.Look at how the product is used, what fails, and which parts support the most important work.
  2. Prioritize the risks.Identify where software, data, or delivery problems create the most pain.
  3. Improve the foundation.Make focused fixes and put clearer checks in place so future changes are easier to manage.

The goal

Software you can change with more confidence, with the most consequential reliability and maintenance issues addressed first.
